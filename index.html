Happy 22nd Birthday, Tamu, my wife.

We found each other at the exact moment neither of us was looking. A comment on Facebook, then a message on Messenger, then a late-night call, and somehow that tiny beginning grew into four years of us.

Out of everything life has given me, you are the one thing I would choose again and again.

12 · 09 · 2025

For so long we were only voices and messages across the distance. But distance never stood a chance, because when the heart is right and the person is right, it simply does not matter how far apart you are.

Then came 12 September 2025, the day I finally saw you. I was so excited my heart would not sit still, and we even turned up in the same colour, as if the universe had planned it. I fell for you at first sight, and I have been falling ever since.

A rickshaw ride, slow walks around Dhanmondi Lake, the metro rail with you by my side. Ordinary days that became my favourite memories because you were in them.

Four years have passed, and my heart still says the same thing: I want this forever. I want every birthday of yours, every rainy evening, every ordinary day, with you beside me.

Wherever life takes us, near or far, I will keep choosing you. May our story end only when life does, still walking together, still holding on.

Happy birthday, my love. My wife. I am yours, always.

Forever yours, Shahed
